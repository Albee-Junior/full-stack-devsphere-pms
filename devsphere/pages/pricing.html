Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans at any time?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you gain immediate access to new features. When you downgrade, changes take effect at the end of your current billing period.

What happens when my free trial ends?

Pro and Team plans include a 14-day free trial. When the trial ends, you'll be moved to the Free plan unless you've upgraded. No charges are made automatically — we never require a credit card to start a trial.

Is there a discount for education or nonprofits?

We offer discounts for verified educational institutions and registered nonprofits. Contact our sales team with your organization's details to learn more.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards and support invoiced billing for annual Business and Enterprise plans. Contact sales for invoice arrangements.

How does billing work for teams?

Team, Business, and Enterprise plans are billed per user. Team and Business seats can be added or removed at any time; billing is prorated for changes made mid-cycle.

Can I get a refund?

We offer refunds within 14 days of your initial purchase for annual plans. Please contact support to discuss your situation.
