Metric definitions

Task completion Percentage of tasks closed (Completed status) during the period, out of tasks that existed at any point in the period.
Project progress Weighted average of completed tasks across all active projects. Milestones are not included.
On-time delivery Percentage of tasks that were completed before or on their due date, out of all tasks with a due date during the period.
Time tracked Total time logged across tasks and projects. Time tracked per person is not visible to Members, only to Admins.
