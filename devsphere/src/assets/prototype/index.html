FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know about getting started.

Is DevSphere really free to start?

Yes. The Free plan gives you a personal workspace, essential task management, basic messaging, and 5 GB of storage — permanently, with no credit card required.

Can I use DevSphere for my team or business?

Pro and Business plans add storage, workspace limits, and paid collaboration features. Business also includes CRM and analytics.

Does DevSphere replace the tools I already use?

DevSphere brings project management, communication, learning, code editing, and productivity into one workspace.

How does the AI assistant use my data?

The current assistant summarizes workspace information. Responses are suggestions and should be checked against your source data.

Can I switch my primary role later?

Yes. Your workspace adapts over time, and you can enable additional tools or change your primary role from workspace settings at any point.

Ready to work in one connected place?

Set up your workspace in under two minutes. Free forever — upgrade only when you need more.
